Albums

Albums that fill themselves

Save any search as an album, and new photos that match join it on their own. Take a wrong one out and it learns what you meant. Copy one into the Photos app whenever you like; only Photosearch's own albums are touched, and only by adding.

Stories

Trips and years, told in photos

Each one plays as a slideshow of its best, most varied moments. Trips are found from where photos were taken (a run of days 75+ km from home). Without a story yet, one is made automatically the first time you play it. For a nicer one, ask Claude: “make a story of our Lisbon trip”. It picks the photos and writes the chapters and captions.
