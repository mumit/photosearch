Stories

Each trip and each year as a slideshow of its best, most varied moments. Trips are found from where photos were taken (a run of days 75+ km from home). Without a story yet, one is made automatically (by day for trips, by month for years) the first time you play it. For a nicer one, ask Claude: “make a story of our Lisbon trip”. It picks the best photos and writes chapters and captions.
